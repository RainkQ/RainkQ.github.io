粒显 LumoGrain 是正在准备发布的 iPhone 照片编辑应用，使用本地物理胶片模拟，把每张原片变成可以反复调整的作品。

保留原片与独立配方，比较相机 JPEG 或默认显影，放大查看颗粒与细节，再导出新的副本。

使用帮助与联系支持

Film development, on your iPhone.

LumoGrain is an iPhone photo editor being prepared for release. Develop RAW photos locally, keep editable recipes, compare against the camera JPEG or a default development, and export a new copy.

The current interface is primarily Simplified Chinese. The app is not yet available on the App Store.
